Rola privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026

In short: Rola does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or user tracking. Your music files, playlists, listening history and settings are stored locally on your device, and Rola does not upload your audio files. Certain features use third-party services, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when a request is made. Rola does not operate its own analytics or user-tracking servers.

Rola is a music player made by Dragoș Mălăescu. It plays audio files you choose from your own device and online radio stations. This policy applies to the web version at dragosmalaescu.github.io/rola and to the Rola app on Google Play. The Android app is a Trusted Web Activity: it opens the same web app from GitHub Pages in your browser engine (for example Chrome), without the address bar. It does not contain a separate player or any additional data collection.

Who is responsible

The controller for the limited processing described here is Dragoș Mălăescu, an independent developer based in Romania. Contact: rola.app@malaescu.ro.

What stays on your device

Everything you add or change in Rola is stored only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • the music files you import, their tags and cover art;
  • favorites, playlists, play counts and recently played items;
  • equalizer settings, theme, language and other preferences.

Rola does not upload your audio files or embedded artwork. When an online feature is enabled or used, limited information such as song title, artist, album name, an audio fingerprint, track duration or radio search terms may be sent to the services listed below. Deleting the library from Rola or clearing the site data deletes the locally stored library and settings permanently. On Android, uninstalling the TWA wrapper may not delete data stored by the browser, so you should also clear Rola's site data from the browser settings. Rola does not provide a cloud backup.

Some of them are located outside the European Economic Area.

Legal basis

Information is sent to the services above only to provide a feature you choose to use, such as identifying a song, looking up titles and artwork or playing a radio station (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, providing the service you request). The technical transmission of your IP address that every internet request involves is based on legitimate interest in making these features work (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Online metadata lookup can be turned off in Settings, and song recognition and radio are used only when you start them.

Retention

Your library, playlists, history and settings stay on your device until you delete them from Rola or clear Rola's site data in your browser. Uninstalling the Android app alone may not remove data stored by the browser. I don't receive or keep any of this data. Third-party services may retain technical logs, such as IP addresses, according to their own policies; I do not control those retention periods.

What I collect

I don't operate a server, an account system or any analytics for Rola, so I don't receive data about you or your listening. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or user tracking, and I don't sell personal data. All of the app's code and fonts are loaded from GitHub Pages; requests to it are handled under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Permissions

Rola accesses only the audio files or folders you select through the system file picker. It does not independently scan your storage and does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. On Android, the app asks for permission to show notifications, which it uses only for the playback controls shown while music is playing. You can refuse or withdraw this permission in your phone's settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing and to data portability. Because Rola keeps your data only on your device, you can view, change or delete it yourself at any time. For any request or question, email rola.app@malaescu.ro. I will reply within one month of receiving your request. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro), or with the authority in your country.

Children

Rola is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Copyright and removal requests

Rola does not host music, radio streams or externally supplied album artwork. Artwork, station logos and stream links are loaded directly from the services listed above. Rights holders may request the removal of a specific artwork, station logo or stream link by email at rola.app@malaescu.ro. Please include the exact URL and evidence that you represent the rights holder. Valid requests will be reviewed and addressed promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or about Rola, email rola.app@malaescu.ro. General questions can also be posted at github.com/dragosmalaescu/rola/issues; please do not post personal information there, because issues are public.
