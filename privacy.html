Rola privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

In short: Rola has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your music files never leave your device. I don't collect or receive any of your personal data. A few features talk to public online services, and those services can see the request and your IP address, as described below.

Rola is a music player made by Dragoș Mălăescu. It plays audio files you choose from your own device and online radio stations. This policy applies to the Rola app on Google Play and to the web version at dragosmalaescu.github.io/rola.

What stays on your device

Everything you add or change in Rola is stored only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • the music files you import, their tags and cover art;
  • favorites, playlists, play counts and recently played items;
  • equalizer settings, theme, language and other preferences.

None of this is uploaded to me or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently. There is no backup in the cloud.

Like any internet request, these services receive your IP address and basic device information such as the browser version. Rola sends no name, email, account, location or advertising identifier. The country used for the radio list is guessed from your phone's time zone, on the device. These services handle requests under their own privacy policies.

What I collect

Nothing. I don't run a server for Rola, and the app contains no analytics, crash reporting, tracking or advertising code. I don't sell or share personal data, because I don't have any.

Permissions

Rola only reads the audio files you pick yourself with the system file picker. It does not scan your storage and does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Children

Rola is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
